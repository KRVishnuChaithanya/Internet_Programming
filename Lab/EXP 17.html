Welcome to VoteOnline

VoteOnline is a secure and user-friendly electronic voting platform designed to simplify the election process. Registered voters can authenticate themselves and cast their votes electronically.

How VoteOnline Works

  1. Voter logs in using Voter ID.
  2. OTP verification is performed.
  3. Voter views the available election.
  4. Voter views candidate information.
  5. Voter selects one candidate.
  6. OTP is generated for voting.
  7. Voter submits the vote.
  8. Vote confirmation is displayed.
