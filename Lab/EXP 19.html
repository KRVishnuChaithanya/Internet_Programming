Online Veterinary Care and Animal Disease Management System

Welcome to VetCure

VetCure is an online veterinary healthcare platform that connects farmers with veterinary doctors. Farmers can submit their animal signs and symptoms using text, images, audio and video and receive solutions from qualified doctors.

How VetCure Works

  1. Farmer registers on the platform.
  2. Farmer logs into the system.
  3. Farmer subscribes to a veterinary service.
  4. Farmer submits animal signs and symptoms.
  5. Doctor receives the complaint.
  6. Doctor analyzes the information.
  7. Doctor provides solution and prescription.
  8. Farmer views the solution and report.

VetCure Services

  • Online veterinary consultation

VetCure Details

About VetCure

VetCure provides farmers with an easy way to communicate animal health problems to veterinary doctors. Farmers can send complaints through text, images, audio or video.

Government CVH Subscription

Farmers can freely subscribe to Government CVH veterinary services.

Private Doctor Subscription

Farmers can subscribe to private veterinary doctors by paying the required consultation fee.

Common Animal Diseases

Foot and Mouth Disease

Symptoms: Fever, mouth blisters and difficulty walking.

Prescription: Veterinary consultation required.

Skin Infection

Symptoms: Skin irritation, hair loss and itching.

Prescription: Proper veterinary examination required.

Digestive Problems

Symptoms: Loss of appetite, diarrhea and weakness.

Prescription: Consult a veterinary doctor.

Respiratory Infection

Symptoms: Coughing, breathing problems and nasal discharge.

Prescription: Veterinary diagnosis recommended.

Livestock Health Management

Preventing Common Animal Diseases

Early identification of symptoms can help prevent the spread of diseases among animals.
