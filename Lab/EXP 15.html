Online Toll Payment and Toll Pass Management System

Welcome to EasyToll

EasyToll is an online toll payment system that allows drivers to pay toll charges in advance. Users can purchase toll passes and eTickets so that they can travel through toll gates without waiting in long queues.

EasyToll Features

  • Online toll payment
  • Advance toll payment
  • eWallet facility
  • Electronic toll tickets
  • Toll pass application
  • Toll pass renewal
  • QR Code for toll pass
  • Vehicle management
  • Transaction history
  • Secure payment
  • Fast toll verification

How EasyToll Works

  1. Create an EasyToll account.
  2. Add money to your eWallet.
  3. Apply for a toll pass.
  4. Buy an eTicket for your journey.
  5. Receive a QR Code.
  6. Show/scan the QR Code at the toll gate.
  7. Toll gate verifies the pass.
  8. Continue your journey without waiting.
